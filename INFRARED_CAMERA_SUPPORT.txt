================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING INFRARED CAMERAS
================================================================================

ISSUE: Camera not detected
SOLUTION:
- Check Windows camera privacy settings
- Ensure camera drivers are installed
- Try different USB ports
- Run camera_test.py to see all available cameras

ISSUE: Poor image quality
SOLUTION:
- Clean camera lens
- Ensure proper distance (18-24 inches)
- Check room temperature (affects IR)
- Adjust camera angle

ISSUE: Face not detected
SOLUTION:
- Remove glasses if causing issues
- Ensure face is centered
- Try different lighting conditions
- Check if IR illuminator is working

ISSUE: Multiple cameras detected
SOLUTION:
- System automatically selects best camera
- Manually specify camera index if needed
- Disable unused cameras in Device Manager
