HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
======================

MINIMUM SPECIFICATIONS (2024):
- Processor: Intel Core i5-6000 series or AMD Ryzen 3 (2.5 GHz)
- Memory: 8 GB RAM (increased for Windows Hello)
- Storage: 5 GB available disk space
- Graphics: DirectX 12 compatible
- Network: Ethernet adapter or Wi-Fi 802.11ac
- Camera: USB 3.0 HD webcam (720p minimum) or Windows Hello camera
- TPM: TPM 2.0 chip (for Windows Hello security)
- Ports: 3x USB ports minimum

RECOMMENDED SPECIFICATIONS (2024):
- Processor: Intel Core i7-8000 series or AMD Ryzen 7 (3.0 GHz+)
- Memory: 16 GB RAM or higher
- Storage: 10 GB available disk space (NVMe SSD preferred)
- Graphics: Dedicated graphics card (for AI processing)
- Network: Gigabit Ethernet or Wi-Fi 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6)
- Camera: Windows Hello infrared camera or 4K webcam
- Biometric: Windows Hello fingerprint reader + face recognition
- TPM: TPM 2.0 with secure boot enabled
- Ports: 4x USB 3.0+ ports for peripherals

BIOMETRIC HARDWARE (2024):
- Webcam: 1080p minimum, 4K recommended, infrared preferred
- Fingerprint Scanner: Windows Hello compatible (required)
- Infrared Camera: For enhanced face recognition (highly recommended)
- Liveness Detection: Hardware-based preferred
- Lighting: Infrared illumination for consistent capture
- Positioning: Stable mounting with adjustable angles
- Security: TPM 2.0 chip for hardware-backed authentication
